About Me

Hello! I'm Sa'ed, an Electrical Power Engineering graduate with a profound interest in bridging the gap between heavy structural power distribution and modern, smart hybrid microgrids.

I execute high-level electrical design operations at Consultancies House for Ultimate Management (CHUM) in Amman, Jordan. My day-to-day involves transforming raw load profiles into perfectly coordinated, clash-free BIM models using advanced engineering simulation tools like ETAP and Navisworks.

I do not just draw lines; I engineer for reality. Whether I am ensuring a hospital layout meets international life-safety regulations or routing Extra-Low Voltage (ELV) networks in commercial spaces, I design in strict compliance with the NFPA, CIBSE, the Saudi Building Code (SBC), and Jordanian National Codes. In environments where precision is non-negotiable, I ensure zero-compromise engineering.
