About Me

Hello! I'm Sa'ed, an Electrical Power Engineer and Electrical Design Engineer based in Amman, Jordan.

I specialize in the design of electrical power, lighting, and ELV systems for commercial, healthcare, residential, and mixed-use developments. My work includes electrical design calculations, technical documentation, tender package preparation, BOQ development, site supervision, inspections, and electrical system design using ETAP, Revit, DIALux, AutoCAD, and Navisworks.

Beyond my professional work, I have a strong interest in low-current systems, networking, structured cabling infrastructure, virtualization, and self-hosted server environments. I maintain a personal home lab where I deploy and manage servers, storage, networking equipment, and self-hosted services.

I am currently expanding my expertise in BIM workflows, advanced electrical design, and intelligent building systems while delivering safe, efficient, and code-compliant designs in accordance with NFPA, CIBSE, the Saudi Building Code (SBC), and Jordanian regulations.
