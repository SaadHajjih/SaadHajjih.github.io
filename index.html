About Me

Hello! I'm Sa'ed, an Electrical Power Engineering graduate with a profound interest in bridging the gap between heavy structural power distribution and modern, smart hybrid microgrids.

Currently, I practice in Amman, Jordan, executing high-level design operations at Consultancies House for Ultimate Management (CHUM). My day-to-day involves transforming raw load profiles into perfectly coordinated, clash-free BIM models using advanced engineering simulation tools.

Whether it is ensuring a hospital meets international medical safety regulations or routing elevator access control networks in luxury residential villas, I thrive in environments where precision is non-negotiable.
